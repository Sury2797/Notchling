## Free and Premium

Free provides basic media transport, one Pomodoro timer and one local scratchpad. Privacy controls, keyboard access, reduced motion and recovery/export remain available. Premium provides the supported extended tool suite for **US$2 per month**, billed monthly. External provider accounts, API charges and AI credits are separate; Notchling includes no cloud sync or unlimited hosted-service allowance.

Development builds are for evaluation. Debug builds visibly expose development access. Release builds start with Free access unless a trusted subscription service supplies a valid, signed Premium entitlement. Configuring a price string or enabling demo mode does not constitute a purchase.

Official Free use and subscribed Premium use are licensed for the customer's own computers and work, subject to the disclosed device allowance and applicable law. The configured service currently permits three verified devices, replaces the oldest when a fourth verifies, requests proof refresh after six hours and allows up to 24 additional hours offline without extending beyond the paid period. Do not resell the app, impersonate an official build or circumvent entitlement controls. Changes to these policies apply prospectively with any notice or cancellation rights required by law.

## Recurring charges and cancellation

Checkout must disclose the monthly recurring price, currency, applicable tax, billing date and cancellation method before confirmation. A subscription renews until cancelled. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the verified already-paid period unless a disclosed refund changes it. Cancellation does not delete local workspace data.

Manage an active subscription through the authenticated billing portal supplied by the configured publisher service. No live portal exists in an unconfigured development build. Never share provider credentials, card details or account recovery tokens through public GitHub issues.

## Refund policy for owner approval

The proposed policy allows a refund request within 14 calendar days of the first paid purchase. Duplicate or unauthorized charges may be reported whenever discovered. Renewals normally have no prorated refund for unused time; applicable withdrawal, refund and other consumer rights override this policy. A verified refund may end Premium access, while workspace preservation/export remains available.

Before payment is enabled, the publisher must provide a private support channel and authenticated refund-request flow, define processing time and publish any regional rules. This proposed refund policy is **not approved or operational** merely because the repository contains it.

## Data and availability

Local workspace files remain on the device. Optional connections and billing requests contact their configured services as described in [Privacy](privacy.md). Keep independent backups. On expiry, Notchling preserves existing material and allows recovery/export; it does not promise ongoing Premium editing without an active entitlement.

Features depend on compatible Windows APIs, external services and explicitly imported data. Optional unavailable media or accounts do not disable Free local tools. Linux desktop support is future work. Windows support is defined in [Windows support](windows-support.md).

The owner must complete and approve them before charging.
