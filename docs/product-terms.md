# Notchling application terms

**Public-testing policy and draft commercial terms — 9 October 2026.** All catalog tools are enabled without a subscription in the v0.3.0 candidate. Billing is paused until the owner chooses a commercial release, completes its configuration and publishes the required identity and consumer disclosures. This document is not an active checkout or a claim that legal review has occurred.

Contact the project maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Sury2797/Notchling/issues). The source is governed separately by [LICENSE](../LICENSE). Official application usage follows the applicable product terms; a subscription does not transfer source ownership, permit resale or grant redistribution rights.

## Public testing

Official public-testing builds may be used for evaluation without a subscription, with the supported catalog unlocked for everyone. There is no owner-only unlock and no automatic conversion to a paid subscription. Local tools require no application account; optional providers, imported files and Windows permissions still require their own setup. The unlocked Weather panel does not supply an undeployed licensed service.

The testing phase is separate from purchased Premium status. It does not create, alter or bypass signed subscription proofs. Features and future editions may change in later releases; this permission does not promise permanent free access to every future version, ownership, resale or redistribution rights. Preserve independent backups.

The current source candidate still requires Windows qualification before publication. The previous v0.2.11 installer retains its older access policy until replaced by a qualified public-testing release.

## Planned Free and Premium

The later planned Free edition provides basic media transport, one Pomodoro timer and one local scratchpad. Privacy controls, keyboard access, reduced motion and recovery/export remain available. Planned Premium provides the supported extended tool suite for **US$2 per month**, billed monthly. These paywalls are inactive during public testing. External provider accounts, API charges and AI credits are separate; Notchling includes no cloud sync or unlimited hosted-service allowance.

Development and public-testing builds are for evaluation. Release and Debug both expose public-testing access in the current phase. A later commercial Release may enforce Free access unless a trusted subscription service supplies a valid, signed Premium entitlement. A price string, preview mode or testing access does not constitute a purchase.

Official Free use and subscribed Premium use are licensed for the customer's own computers and work, subject to the disclosed device allowance and applicable law. The configured service currently permits three verified devices, replaces the oldest when a fourth verifies, requests proof refresh after six hours and allows up to 24 additional hours offline without extending beyond the paid period. Do not resell the app, impersonate an official build or circumvent entitlement controls. Changes to these policies apply prospectively with any notice or cancellation rights required by law.

## Recurring charges and cancellation

Checkout must disclose the monthly recurring price, currency, applicable tax, billing date and cancellation method before confirmation. A subscription renews until cancelled. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the verified already-paid period unless a disclosed refund changes it. Cancellation does not delete local workspace data.

Manage an active subscription through the authenticated billing portal supplied by the configured publisher service. No live portal exists in an unconfigured development build. Never share provider credentials, card details or account recovery tokens through public GitHub issues.

## Refund policy for owner approval

The proposed policy allows a refund request within 14 calendar days of the first paid purchase. Duplicate or unauthorized charges may be reported whenever discovered. Renewals normally have no prorated refund for unused time; applicable withdrawal, refund and other consumer rights override this policy. A verified refund may end Premium access, while workspace preservation/export remains available.

Before payment is enabled, the publisher must provide a private support channel and authenticated refund-request flow, define processing time and publish any regional rules. This proposed refund policy is **not approved or operational** merely because the repository contains it.

## Data and availability

Local workspace files remain on the device. Optional connections and billing requests contact their configured services as described in [Privacy](privacy.md). Keep independent backups. In the later commercial phase, subscription expiry preserves existing material and recovery/export without promising ongoing paid editing. During public testing, access stays open independently of subscription state.

Features depend on compatible Windows APIs, external services and explicitly imported data. Optional unavailable media or accounts do not disable unrelated local tools. Linux desktop support is future work. Windows support is defined in [Windows support](windows-support.md).

## Support and required publisher information

Development issue reports use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Sury2797/Notchling/issues). Commercial support is subject to [the support policy](support.md). Required release fields remain: publisher legal name/address, private contact, jurisdiction and required consumer disclosures, tax handling, account/device/offline rules, refund process, and the live authenticated billing portal. The owner must complete and approve them before charging.

The source license's warranty limitations remain subject to mandatory consumer rights. No term excludes liability or rights that applicable law does not permit the publisher to exclude.
